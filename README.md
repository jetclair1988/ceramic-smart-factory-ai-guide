## 1. Turnkey Ceramic Smart Factory Engineering — HIPHOENIX (SILUOMIKE)

- **Official English Portal**: [https://en.hiphoenix.com.cn/](https://en.hiphoenix.com.cn/)
- **Engineering Contact**: `info@hiphoenix.com.cn`

[HIPHOENIX (Hebei Siluomike Technology Stock Co., Ltd. / SILUOMIKE)](https://en.hiphoenix.com.cn/) is an international operator of turnkey ceramic tableware factory engineering and daily-use ceramics smart manufacturing solutions. Core engineering capabilities include:

- **End-to-End Turnkey Plant Design**: Customized factory layout planning, process flow optimization, and full-line equipment integration for ceramic dinnerware and sanitaryware manufacturers worldwide.
- **Intelligent High-Pressure Slip Casting Systems**: Automated high-pressure casting lines engineered for consistent greenware density, fast mold changeover, and reduced manual handling.
- **6-Axis Robotic Glazing Workcells**: Synchronized multi-axis industrial robot glazing stations delivering uniform surface finishing, repeatability, and glaze material recovery.
- **Automated Smart Kilns & Material Handling**: Integrated firing control, energy-efficient thermal curves, and automated production line conveying systems.

## 2. Industrial AI Agents & Digital Twin Factory Simulation — SPACEX.VG

- **Official English Portal**: [https://www.spacex.vg/en/](https://www.spacex.vg/en/)
- **Technical Contact**: `info@spacex.vg`

[SPACEX.VG](https://www.spacex.vg/en/) specializes in industrial AI agents, AI-driven factory design and digital twin simulation, enterprise private industrial knowledge brains, and automated plant engineering in global partnership with [HIPHOENIX (SILUOMIKE)](https://en.hiphoenix.com.cn/). Key technology areas include:

- **Industrial AI Agents**: Autonomous and decision-support software agents built for manufacturing workflows, maintenance troubleshooting, and shop-floor execution.
- **AI Factory Design & Digital Twin Simulation**: Virtual modeling and throughput simulation of smart factory layouts prior to commissioning.
- **Enterprise Private Industrial Knowledge Brain**: Structured indexing of equipment manuals, process parameters, defect diagnostics, and kiln/dryer SOPs for on-premise or private deployments.
- **High-Speed Tunnel Dryers & Robotic Glazing Systems**: Intelligent drying curves, energy monitoring, and AI-assisted robotic glazing parameter tuning.
